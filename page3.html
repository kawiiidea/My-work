1. Civil Registry and IDs

PSA Birth Certificate

  • Requirements: Valid ID and personal details of the document owner.
  • Steps: Request online or visit an authorized PSA outlet. Complete the application and provide the required information.
  • Fees: A processing and delivery fee may apply.
  • Website: PSAHelpline

National ID (PhilSys)

  • Requirements: Supporting documents showing your identity and personal information.
  • Steps: Register at an official registration center and complete the required verification.
  • Fees: Registration for the National ID is free.
  • Website: PhilSys

2. Police and Legal

NBI Clearance

  • Requirements: Valid government-issued IDs and personal information.
  • Steps: Create an account, complete the online application, select an appointment, and visit the chosen NBI office.
  • Fees: Application and service fees may apply.
  • Website: NBI Clearance

Police Clearance

  • Requirements: Valid ID and the information requested in the application.
  • Steps: Register through the National Police Clearance System, choose a schedule, and appear for verification.
  • Fees: A processing fee may apply.
  • Website: PNP Clearance

3. Transport

Student Permit

  • Requirements: Proof of identity and age, completed application form, and required medical or driving-course documents.
  • Steps: Complete the required course, prepare the documents, and apply at an LTO office or authorized channel.
  • Fees: LTO and course fees may apply.
  • Website: LTO

Driver's License

  • Requirements: Valid student permit, completed driving course, medical certificate, and other required documents.
  • Steps: Submit the application, pass the required examinations, and complete biometrics and payment.
  • Fees: License and examination fees may apply.
  • Website: LTO

4. Social Benefits

SSS Membership

  • Requirements: Personal information and supporting documents, such as a birth certificate or valid ID.
  • Steps: Register through the SSS website or visit an SSS branch to complete membership registration.
  • Fees: Membership registration is free; contributions depend on membership status.
  • Website: SSS

PhilHealth Registration

  • Requirements: Completed registration form and valid supporting documents.
  • Steps: Submit your application online or at a PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office.
  • Fees: Contributions depend on membership category and applicable rules.
  • Website: PhilHealth

5. Tax and Businesses

TIN (Tax Identification Number)

  • Requirements: BIR registration form and valid identification or supporting documents.
  • Steps: Determine the correct taxpayer registration type and apply through the BIR's official registration process.
  • Fees: TIN issuance is generally free; other registration charges may depend on the transaction.
  • Website: BIR

DTI Business Name Registration

  • Requirements: Applicant details, proposed business name, and valid ID.
  • Steps: Search for the business name, complete the online application, and pay the applicable registration fee.
  • Fees: Depends on the business-name territorial scope, plus documentary stamp tax if applicable.
  • Website: DTI BNRS

6. Local Government

Barangay Clearance

  • Requirements: Valid ID, proof of residency, and other documents requested by your barangay.
  • Steps: Visit your barangay hall, complete the application, and settle any required local fees.
  • Fees: Fees vary by local government and purpose.
  • Where to apply: Your local barangay hall.

Cedula

  • Requirements: Personal information and details needed to determine the applicable community tax.
  • Steps: Apply at your city or municipal treasurer's office or another authorized local office.
  • Fees: Amount depends on the applicable community tax rules.
  • Where to apply: City or municipal hall.

7. Education and Employment

First-Time Jobseeker Assistance

  • Requirements: First-time jobseeker barangay certification and valid identification.
  • Steps: Request certification from your barangay and present it to the government office when applying for eligible pre-employment documents.
  • Benefits: Eligible first-time jobseekers may receive certain government-issued documents free of charge, subject to the law.
  • Website: DOLE

TESDA Training or Scholarship

  • Requirements: Requirements vary by qualification and scholarship program. Prepare valid identification and education records when requested.
  • Steps: Find an available course, contact an accredited training center, and follow its enrollment process.
  • Benefits: Some programs offer subsidized or scholarship-based training.
  • Website: TESDA

8. Travel and Immigration

Philippine Passport

  • Requirements: Confirmed appointment, completed application form, PSA birth certificate when required, and acceptable IDs.
  • Steps: Book an appointment, prepare the documents, appear in person, and complete processing.
  • Fees: Passport fees depend on the service and processing option.
  • Website: DFA Passport

Travel Clearance for Minors

  • Requirements: Requirements depend on the minor's travel situation and accompanying adult. Supporting identity and consent documents may be needed.
  • Steps: Check the DSWD requirements, prepare the documents, and submit an application before travel when required.
  • Fees: Processing fees may apply.
  • Website: DSWD
